what we do

Dozens of meetups teach people to code. We are doing something a little different — building a local community of friendly, creative, highly-motivated programmers. We're open to every language, every level, every kind of project.

The format is self-study with advice and mentorship from the more experienced members. We can help you with study plans and guidance — just ask at a meetup or in the forum.

bootcamp supplement

Attend as a self-directed bootcamp supplement or alternative. Experienced programmers can offer mentorship on project-based learning.
