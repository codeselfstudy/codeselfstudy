Study programming, together.

Why people come

Community

Show up, meet people who care about what you're working on, leave less alone.

Mentorship

Experienced members help beginners. Bring a question, ask for a study plan.

Networking

Many members have found jobs through introductions and tips from the group.

What we do

Dozens of groups teach people to code. We instead build a local community of friendly, creative, highly-motivated programmers. We're open to every language and every level. The format is self-study with guidance from more experienced members.
