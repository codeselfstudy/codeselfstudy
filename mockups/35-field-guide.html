Genus · Community · No. 01

The Bay Area programmer.

Programmator congregans var. Berkeley

Common throughout the Bay Area, particularly in Berkeley. Gregarious; observed in groups of five or more on Saturday mornings. Plumage varies — Python, JavaScript, Rust, Haskell, Clojure, and several dozen lesser dialects have been documented in the same flock.

The species is best identified by its habit of returning, week after week, to the same study site, where it sits beside other members of the flock and quietly works on whatever it is learning that morning.

Habitat note

Dozens of groups will teach you to code. We build a local community of highly-motivated programmers instead. The format is self-study with mentorship — show up at a meetup, bring a question or a project, get help from the regulars. No curriculum, no gatekeeping, no bootcamp pitch.
