codeselfstudy.org / 5,247 members / since 2014

Study code. Together. In person.

A friendly programming community of 5,000+ people in Berkeley and the San Francisco Bay Area. All languages, all levels welcome. The format is self-study with mentorship — show up at a meetup, bring a question or a project, get help from the regulars.

▸ Join the community
Statement

Dozens of groups will teach you to code. We build a local community of friendly, highly-motivated programmers instead. No curriculum, no gatekeeping, no bootcamp pitch.
