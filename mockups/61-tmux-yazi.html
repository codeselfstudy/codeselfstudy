Code Self Study

A friendly programming community in the SF Bay Area — est. 2014. 5,000+ members. All languages, all levels. In person and online.

what we do

  • build a local community of motivated programmers
  • open to every language & level — bring a project
  • self-study with mentorship from experienced members
  • works as a bootcamp supplement — or alternative
